Refined Coastal Hospitality

Where Ocean Breeze Meets Culinary Craftsmanship

“Fine dining should feel like an intimate conversation between the sea, the harvest, and the guest.”

Tucked along the tranquil oceanfront of Beach Road in Negombo, MiniKitchen is an ode to Sri Lanka’s storied maritime heritage. Here, every dish is an artful celebration of freshness—from sweet lagoon crabs and spiced jumbo cuttlefish to aromatic Ceylon curries gently stewed over slow embers.

Our culinary philosophy is rooted in authenticity and restraint: letting prime local ingredients speak through harmonious spices, accompanied by attentive, intuitive hospitality that anticipates every guest preference.

Heritage & Provenance

The Rhythm of Negombo Beach Road

For centuries, Negombo has been recognized as the epicentre of Sri Lanka’s coastal fishing culture. Here, the Indian Ocean meets the sheltered waters of the lagoon, creating an ecosystem rich in colossal mud crabs, king prawns, and tender cuttlefish.

MiniKitchen was established with a singular devotion: to preserve heirloom coastal recipes that have graced Sri Lankan family tables for generations, while elevating them with refined culinary balance and contemporary fine-dining presentation.

Lagoon Outriggers

Direct partnerships with independent artisanal fishermen in Negombo.

Slow Embers

Curries gently simmered in earthen clay pots for deep flavor development.

No Compromise

Unrefined sea salt, stone-ground chilies, and fresh hand-squeezed coconut milk.

Warm Grace

Intuitive, discrete hospitality tailored to each visiting guest.
