Our Story

Coastal Hospitality, Authentic Flavors

MiniKitchen started as a small beachside kitchen with a passion for Negombo's vibrant culinary tradition. We source seafood directly from local Negombo lagoon fishermen every morning and grind our spices by hand.

Come for the fresh sea breeze, stay for the rich crab curry, sizzling kottu, and warm Sri Lankan smiles.
